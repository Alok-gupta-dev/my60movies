MY 60
MOVIES

1

The Shawshank Redemption

True Story

Do qaidiyon ki dosti, umeed aur insaaniyat ki kahani. Andar ki duniya ko bahar se zyada insaani dikhata hai.

2

Lion

True Story

Ek 5 saal ka bachcha bichad jaata hai apni maa se. 25 saal baad Google Earth se apna ghar dhundhta hai. Sachchi aur rula dene wali kahani.

3

Unbroken

True Story

WWII mein ek Olympic runner ka survival — plane crash, samudra, war camp. Haarna usne seekha hi nahi.

4

Scam 1992

True Story

Harshad Mehta ki asli kahani — Bombay ke streets se Dalal Street tak ka safar. India ka sabse bada stock market scam.

5

Matka King

True Story

Mumbai ke gambling underworld ka raja — Ratan Khatri ki sachchi kahani jo crores ke saath khelta tha.

6

12 Years a Slave

True Story

Ek azaad aadmi ko pakad ke gulam bana diya jaata hai. 12 saal ki barbarism aur phir azaadi ki sachchi kahani.

7

The Social Network

True Story

Zuckerberg ne ek idea se duniya badal di — aur saath mein dosti bhi tod di. Facebook banana itna simple nahi tha.

8

The Imitation Game

True Story

Alan Turing ne Nazi ka code toda aur WWII jitaya. Usi insaan ke saath duniya ne kya kiya — yeh sochne wala hai.

9

Ramanujan

True Story

Tamil Nadu ke ek garib ladke ne mathematics ki duniya ko hila diya. Koi degree nahi, bas dimag aur junoon tha.

10

Dangal

True Story

11

The Dark Knight

Adventure

12

Interstellar

Adventure

13

Inception

Adventure

Sapne ke andar sapna — dimag ki layers mein ghusna aur idea plant karna. Nolan ka masterpiece, ek baar mein samajh nahi aayega.

14

Tenet

Adventure

Time ulta chal raha hai aur duniya bachani hai. Nolan ka sabse complex thriller — baar baar dekhoge tab bhi kuch nayi cheez milegi.

15

Apex

Adventure

16

Stolen

18

Jurassic Park

Adventure

Dinosaurs wapas aa gaye hain ek island pe. Excitement aur darr ek saath — childhood ka classic adventure.

19

Into the Wild

Adventure

20

Life of Pi

Adventure

Titanic

Romantic

The Notebook

Romantic

Photograph

Udaan

Romantic

29

A Beautiful Mind

Taxi Driver
